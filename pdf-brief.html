The Methodology

Mirror Reflection & Response

The Mirror Reflection & Response (MRR) Methodology is a high-responsivity framework designed to navigate institutional risk and maximize cognitive behavioral shifts in justice-impacted youth.

The 3-Tier Intervention Loop

  • Tier 1: Cinematic Entry – Utilizing high-production film to bypass institutional resistance and establish a shared narrative "hook."
  • Tier 2: Mirror Reflection – Guided introspection where youth identify their "Mirror Self," analyzing dynamic risk factors and triggers.
  • Tier 3: Response & Action – Moving from reflection to prosocial behavior, drafting the "Next Chapter" for successful community reentry.
