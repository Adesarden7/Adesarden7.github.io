MRR Method

Mirror Reflection & Response (MRR) is a three-tier intervention loop designed for high-risk populations.

Tier 1: Cinematic Entry
Establish rapport and de-escalate institutional resistance through narrative storytelling.

Tier 2: Mirror Reflection
Guided sessions where youth identify triggers and "Mirror Selves".

Tier 3: Response & Action
Prosocial goal setting and "Next Chapter" drafting for community reentry.
