Trauma-Informed, Accountability-Centered Facilitation

Axel Desarden’s approach is emotionally grounded, direct, reflective, and relational. Rather than relying on generic motivation, his work uses narrative as an entry point to examine personal history, beliefs, emotional awareness, decisions, behavioral patterns, and future possibilities.

Designed to complement existing Risk-Need-Responsivity (RNR) and Cognitive Behavioral Intervention (CBI) programming, the work creates a structured space for honest self-examination without reducing participants to their past choices.

Mirror Reflection & Response (MRR) Methodology

A structured, four-part cognitive-behavioral intervention framework designed to complement existing institutional programming and deepen participant engagement.

01

The Mirror

Examining personal history, distorted perceptions, and survival responses through guided self-reflection.

02

The Pen

Expressive writing and narrative reconstruction to reframe personal identity and internal dialogue.

03

The Mic

Accountability-centered dialogue and verbal ownership in a supportive group facilitation setting.

04

The Vision

Future visioning, actionable decision-making, and practical planning for sustainable community reintegration.

Cinematic Catalyst

Distorted Perception of Innocence

A 40-minute autobiographical narrative film created to serve as an initial cinematic catalyst for engagement and reflection. The film opens the door to facilitated dialogue, helping participants confront distorted self-perceptions before moving into structured MRR exercises.
